      HOW TO PLAY

      THE IDEA

      Two fleets hide on two grids. You can't see the enemy's ships and they can't see yours. Take turns calling shots at squares; the first to sink every enemy ship wins.

      1. SET UP

      • Grid size — bigger grids mean longer games. 10×10 is the classic.
      • Fleet — which ships are in play. Classic is 1 Carrier (5), 1 Battleship (4), 2 Cruisers (3), 1 Destroyer (2). The number is how many squares the ship covers.
      • Enemy commander — how clever the computer is. Start with Cadet.
      • Opponent — play the computer, or create a room and send the link to a friend.

      2. DEPLOY YOUR FLEET

      • Drag each ship from the Dry Dock onto your grid. Green preview = OK, red = overlaps or off the grid.
      • Tap a placed ship to select it, tap again (or press Rotate) to turn it.
      • Ships can touch but can't overlap. In a hurry? Press Randomize.
      • When every ship is placed, press Engage.

      3. BATTLE

      On your turn, tap any square on Enemy Waters (the top grid). The result appears instantly:

      Miss — nothing there. Try somewhere else. Hit — part of a ship. Ships are straight lines, so try the squares next to it. Sunk — every square of that ship is hit. The fleet tracker crosses it off.

      Then the enemy fires at Your Fleet (the lower grid). Squares can only be shot once.

      4. WINNING

      Sink all enemy ships before they sink yours. Your accuracy and shot count are shown at the end.

      BEGINNER TIPS

      • After a hit, fire at the four neighbouring squares until you find the ship's direction, then follow the line.
      • Searching in a checkerboard pattern finds every ship of length 2 or more with half the shots.
      • Keep track of which enemy ships are still afloat: if only the Destroyer (2) is left, tiny gaps can't hide anything.
      • Spread your own ships out; clustered fleets are found fast once one is hit.

      CONTROLS

      • Mouse or touch: drag, tap, click.
      • Keyboard: Tab to a square, Enter or Space to fire.
